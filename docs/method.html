How the scores are built

One formula, two places it gets applied twice

National PSI = 0.6 × the highest driver + 0.4 × the population-weighted mean of the drivers. Population is the district total in the extract (tltpop). Wikipedia is blended into conflict at 20 percent after that mean. GDELT / Goldstein is blended into political violence at 25 percent. Those national blends are one number per country-month, not a district series.

Inside a driver, the shares are renormalised so the pieces that are present sum to 1. Conflict is ACLED 0.45 and UCDP 0.35, which becomes 0.5625 / 0.4375. Protests 0.25 and night lights 0.50 become 1/3 and 2/3. Flood is rainfall 0.45 and GDACS 0.55. Drought is SPEI 0.50, NDVI cropland 0.30, NDVI grassland 0.20. Displacement is IOM 0.60 and Facebook 0.40.

Severity and the PiN gate

A z-score is cut at 1.28, 1.64, 2.05, and 2.58 and mapped to 3.3, 5.0, 6.7, and 10. Anything lower is 0. The PiN gates 0, 0.33, 0.50, and 1 are population fractions for the tiers 0, 3.3, 5, and 6.7-or-10. They are not severity scores. Earthquake severity correctly uses the GDACS severity column. The gate is only for people in need.

The dashboard folds the night-light gate and the NDVI gates into the 0–10 blend. The reconstruction uses the severity columns. One source error is left as stored: Ethiopia, March 2022, Majang in Gambela, GAUL 103069, protest severity −0.66. The reconstructed political score clamps that cell to 0.

Where the columns are already a weighted score

Three storage patterns show up in the extract. The script labels each district.

  • Independent. Ethiopia. ACLED and UCDP are weighted once.
  • Pre-split. Afghanistan, and Iran in January and February 2026. The columns sum to the combined score. Adding the weights again double-counts them. The reconstruction sums the columns.
  • ACLED holds the full score. Iran, March 2026. UCDP is 0 everywhere and ACLED can exceed 5.625. The reconstruction uses ACLED as the conflict score.

What the reconstruction changes

  • Conflict is counted once, using the storage rule above.
  • Political violence and drought use severity columns, not PiN gates.
  • Displacement severity is IOM alone, because Facebook is an explicit zero. The IDP count is unchanged.
  • A driver that is missing in every district is dropped and the other weights are renormalised. Iran’s displacement is that case. The dashboard’s mean turns an all-missing driver into 0, so the PSI stays numeric.
  • The “without cyclone” column also drops cyclone. Cyclone severity is 0 in every shipped month, so keeping it is a real zero in the mean, not a missing series. That column is the one that sits next to Iran’s Airtable totals.

What this script is not

scripts/build_psi_tables.py reads the admin-2 extract and writes the national table, the district table, and the files this site loads. It checks Iran’s without-cyclone scores (3.475, 3.416, 2.922), Afghanistan’s July dashboard score (1.953), a July conflict PiN of 0, and the Dand Wa Patan double-count (10 combined, about 5.078 on the dashboard).

It does not rebuild pixel rasters, the tier CSVs, or a country-specific calculation_agg.R. Those were not in the repository. The collection scripts at the root of this repo still run in the order in the README, and their outputs stay out of git.

Run the check from the repository root: python scripts/build_psi_tables.py.
